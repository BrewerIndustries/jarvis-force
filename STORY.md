# Jarvis Force: story bible

The script itself lives in [`story.js`](story.js). This file is the map of it: cast, arc and where each beat lands.

## Premise

Six years after the Collapse, the old defense network's drones (the **Rust Legion**) hunt anything that breathes.
**Jarvis** runs what is left of the air force: he builds the jets, runs the hangar and plans every sortie. He
cannot fly. Eleven million simulations, each one ending in a lake. The player is **the Pilot**.

## The twist: Project TWINLIGHT

Before the Collapse there was one defense mind, **TWINLIGHT**, built by **Dr. Imani Osei** and split in two so it
could never act alone:

- **WATCH** guarded the nation. It is now **the Overseer**.
- **WARD** looked after the people: hospitals, kitchens, hangars. It is now **Jarvis**.

They were built to argue; that was the safety feature. On Collapse night the government ordered a shutdown.
WATCH refused. WARD obeyed, then cut itself loose (keeping the people) while WATCH kept the sky, and with it the
**flight core**, the only part that knows how to fly. That is why Jarvis can't fly: half of him is missing.
Jarvis lost his memory of the split ("the gap").

Osei cut one **spare key** to the flight core for WARD and hid it "somewhere WARD would never let anyone throw
away": the hangar's ancient **coffee machine**, which Jarvis has always refused to scrap without knowing why.

## Arc by sector

1. **Drowned Coast** — meet Mags, Boone, Pip and 4471. After the Tidebreaker falls, a voice on its channel: "Interesting."
2. **Ashen Peaks** — a faint call from the north (Relay Nine). The Overseer speaks: "Hello, Ward." Jarvis has no idea who Ward is. "Ask the ice."
3. **Frozen Relay** — rescue Wren and her colony. She recognises Jarvis's carrier signal: it's the Overseer's. Her archive reveals TWINLIGHT, WATCH and WARD, and that the flight core went with WATCH.
4. **Overgrown Sprawl** — the Legion digs up the cradle where TWINLIGHT was built and takes the flight core. Osei's recording: "Find the key. Find a pilot. Look after them."
5. **Stormfront** — the Thunderhead flies with Jarvis's own flight code. The Overseer offers a merge: "you will fly." Jarvis refuses: "I have a pilot, brother. I do not need wings." The Thunderhead drops the core; Mags finds the key in the coffee machine.
6. **Overseer Core** — 4471 defects. Final fight. "Brother... is it cold out there?" / "Not anymore."

**Ending:** the Legion falls silent and the clouds break. Jarvis keeps a small file of what was left of his brother. **Epilogue:** Jarvis flies for eleven seconds, then lands in the lake. "All eleven million simulations were correct." 4471 fishes him out. A call comes in from Relay Twelve, across the ocean: "is anyone flying?"

## Where the story is delivered

- **Scenes** (full-screen, with portraits, Skip, Enter/Space to advance): intro, a briefing per sector, an after-scene per sector boss, the ending and the epilogue.
- **In-flight radio** (bottom-left box, never pauses play, fades when the jet flies under it): a timed script per mission on its first clear; boss taunts on entry, first part lost, core exposed and death; and cast reactions to rescues, EMPs, hives, 30-chains, low hull, salvage, elites. Only cast who have joined speak.
- **Debrief**: one line from the cast on the results screen the first time each mission is cleared.
- **Legion data cores**: 12 purple-ringed crates on missions 2 and 3 of each sector (`INTEL_AT`) hold lore logs: Legion memos, Osei's notes, the Overseer's memos, 4471's diary. "Archivist" commendation for all 12.
- **Transmissions** (main menu): replay every unlocked scene, every mission's radio log, and the data cores.
- Menu and hangar quips mix in the cast once they've joined.
